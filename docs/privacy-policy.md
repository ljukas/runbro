# RunBro — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 October 2026

This policy explains what information the RunBro app ("RunBro", "the app")
handles on iPhone and Android, why, where it is kept and how to remove it. It
applies to the app as published on the App Store and Google Play.

## Who is responsible

RunBro is made by Lukas Lindqvist, an individual developer in Sweden, who is the
data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation
(GDPR). For any privacy question or request, email
**runbro@lukaslindqvist.se**.

## In short

- RunBro has **no backend, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no
  tracking**. The developer never receives your runs, your location or any
  other information about you.
- Everything the app records is stored **on your phone only**.
- Data leaves your phone only when **you** choose it (Apple Health or Health
  Connect, sharing an export, your phone's own backup) and when the app loads
  **map images** from Apple or Google to show a route (see below).
- RunBro does not sell, rent or share your data with anyone.

## Information the app handles on your phone

| What | Why | Where it is kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runs: date, duration, intervals, pauses, distance, pace | To guide your programme and show your history | The app's database on your phone |
| Precise location (GPS), only while a run is in progress | To measure distance and pace and draw your route | The app's database on your phone |
| Air-pressure (barometer) readings and step counts, only while a run is in progress | To estimate elevation gain and improve the run summary | The app's database on your phone |
| Your settings and programme progress | To remember your choices | The app's storage on your phone |

RunBro does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or any other
identifying information.

## Permissions

The app asks for these permissions, each only when the feature that needs it is
first used, and works without the optional ones:

- **Location (while using the app).** Used only during a run. On Android a
  notification is shown while a run records, so it can keep measuring with the
  screen off. RunBro does not request background ("all the time") location and
  never tracks you outside a run.
- **Physical activity (Android) / Motion & Fitness (iPhone).** Used only during
  a run, for step counts and elevation.
- **Health Connect (Android) / Apple Health (iPhone), write only.** Lets the app
  save finished runs. RunBro requests no read access and never reads your health
  data.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your phone's settings; the
related feature then stops working.

## When information leaves your phone

**Maps.** To show a recorded route on a map, the app downloads map images from
the map provider: Google Maps on Android and Apple Maps on iPhone. Your route
line is drawn on your phone and is not sent, but the provider receives the
requests for the area being shown, together with technical information such as
your IP address, device model and operating-system version. The Google Maps
SDK also sends Google crash reports and a pseudonymous usage identifier. That
information is processed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies:
[Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**Health Connect and Apple Health, if you allow it.** Finished runs are written
as workouts, with their duration, distance, intervals and route. From then on
the health store governs that data like any other workout saved there,
including syncing it and sharing it with other apps that you have separately
given access to. You can turn this off at any time in Health Connect or the
Health app.

**Exports you share.** A run's detail screen can export that run's recorded
data (GPS points and altitude readings) as a text file. It goes only where you
send it through your phone's share sheet.

**Your phone's backup.** The app's data is included in your phone's own backup
(iCloud on iPhone, Google's backup on Android), under your account and that
provider's terms. RunBro has no access to these backups.

**Spoken cues** use your phone's built-in speech engine.

## How your data is protected

The data stays in the app's private storage, which your phone's operating
system protects from other apps, and is covered by your phone's lock-screen and
device encryption. Because nothing is sent to the developer, there is no server
holding a copy that could be breached.

## How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. Uninstalling the app, or
clearing its data in your phone's settings, permanently deletes everything the
app has stored. Runs already saved to Health Connect or Apple Health stay there
until you delete them in that app, and backups follow the backup provider's
retention.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your
personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because the
developer holds no data about you, all of it is on your phone and in your
control, and you can delete it as described above. If you have a question or
concern, email runbro@lukaslindqvist.se. You also have the right to complain to
a data-protection authority; in Sweden that is the Swedish Authority for Privacy
Protection (IMY, [imy.se](https://www.imy.se)).

## Children

RunBro is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process
information about them.
